# Idea dependencies and task blockers live in separate junctions

The planning graph (Ideas blocking Ideas, traversed by the recursive planner to decide what to grill next) and the dispatch graph (Tasks blocking Tasks, traversed by the orchestrator to decide dispatch order and waves) encode different concepts: functional readability vs. codebase-state ordering. They are stored in two separate junction tables, idea_dependencies and task_blockers, with no cross-graph edges allowed (no Task->Idea, no Idea->Task). Rejected: a polymorphic dependencies table with (kind,id) on each endpoint, because conflating the two graphs into one table forces every reader to disambiguate by kind and muddies the meaning of a row. Cost accepted: two read paths for any future consumer that wants a unified graph view; the conceptual cleanliness pays for itself on every write, and no such unified view exists today.
