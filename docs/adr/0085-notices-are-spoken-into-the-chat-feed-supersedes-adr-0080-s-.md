# Notices are spoken into the chat feed; supersedes ADR-0080's chat-shows-only-threads clause

ADR-0080 split the surfaces after raiseActionQueueItem fire-and-forgot a chat_threads row per action-queue raise and drowned the conversation list: the Bell got Alerts+Notices, chat got threads only. ADR-0077 then required that all Steward and Reflector output reach the human only as messages in the unified first-person chat, so the two ADRs already disagreed. Decision: Notices are spoken into the chat feed as zero-token first-person messages that never take the floor, while Alerts stay in the Bell and are never auto-converted. ADR-0080's actual concern is upheld rather than reversed, because nothing auto-mints a thread — only a human opens a Subject, so the durable-thread churn that motivated ADR-0080 cannot recur. Alerts remain derived and mutation-cleared (ADR-0048/0054); Notices remain ack-cleared (ADR-0079). Trade-off: the two-plane separation ADR-0080 introduced is now partial rather than absolute, and the cost of a bad Notice is paid in the conversation the operator cannot navigate away from, which is why a Notice kind ships only once it has a plain sentence a non-expert understands.
