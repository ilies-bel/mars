# Arc is the sole writer of task_blockers; Linker remains the sole deriver of lexical-overlap edges (ADR-0006 clarification)

ADR-0006 established the policy split: the deterministic Linker is the sole *deriver* of lexical-overlap blocker edges, while causal writers (fix-task pipeline, manual mars block, idea-promote transfer, slicer) write confirmed rows as before. This clarification extends ADR-0052's sole-writer boundary to the task_blockers edge table: the Arc aggregate is now the sole *writer* of all task_blockers rows. The Linker's deriver role is unchanged — it still decides which lexical-overlap edges to create, but the actual SQL INSERT is routed through Arc.addPendingReviewBlockers instead of raw SQL. Causal writers similarly route through Arc.addBlocker, Arc.clearBlockers, Arc.removeBlocker, or Arc.transferProposalEdges. Migration writes inside migrateQueueSchema are the only exemption, honored via arch-guard marker comments. ADR-0006's policy split (deriver vs writer) is preserved and sharpened: the Linker owns WHAT edges exist; Arc owns HOW they are persisted.
