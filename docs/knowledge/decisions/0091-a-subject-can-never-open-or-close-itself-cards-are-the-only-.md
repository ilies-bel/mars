# A Subject can never open or close itself; Cards are the only door in and out

Context: Subjects could open with a null objective and a null terminal_event_type (chat-store.ts:79-95) and then linger until closed by hand, while the only sanctioned way to open one — PreloadedResponse.target {type:'subthread', title} — could declare neither a goal nor an exit. Decision: every Subject opens with exactly one Objective and one Terminal condition, both mandatory, and when that condition is satisfied it raises a Closure card instead of closing; the main thread likewise answers free text rather than opening Subjects, so a Card is the only door in and out. Why: unilateral auto-close was rejected because a Subject cannot know whether the operator has a follow-up, and the cost of lingering is one gesture whereas the cost of closing wrongly is a lost thread; this is not the operator close verb ADR-0048 banned, because closing sets closed_at and the awaiting-closure state therefore evaporates by genuine entity mutation.
